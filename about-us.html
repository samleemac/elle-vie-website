OUR STORY

We are more than a beauty salon, we are a place where you can enjoy and relax

Elle Vie Hair & Beauty was launched in November 2013, situated in the heart of Belper and in the rolling hills of Derbyshire.

Lyndi and Vicki have over 10+ years of experience as salon owners and beauticians. During this time they have been able to grown and learn a range of high quality services, something they have been offering to the community for over a decade.

To read more about our story, take a look at our timeline below.
