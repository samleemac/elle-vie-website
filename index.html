OUR STORY

We are more than a salon, we are a place where you can enjoy and relax

Elle Vie Hair & Beauty was launched in November 2013, situated in the heart of Belper and in the rolling hills of Derbyshire.

Lyndi and Vicki have over 10+ years of experience as salon owners, hair dressers and beauty therapists. During this time they have been able to grown and learn a range of high quality services, something they have been offering to the community for over a decade.
